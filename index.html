What are no-touch instruments?

No‑touch instruments are digital musical interfaces that allow users to play, shape and explore sound without any physical contact. They rely on hand‑tracking, computer vision and gesture‑based interaction to translate movement into expressive musical actions. These instruments are designed to be accessible, intuitive and inclusive, enabling meaningful musical experiences for everyone — including individuals with motor or neurological disabilities, special educational needs and diverse learning profiles.

Handscapes

Handscapes is a family of touchless digital musical instruments and interactive environments that transform hand and body movements into sound, visuals, and immersive experiences. A family of touchless instruments and interactive experiences designed for:

  • Materials – creating participatory artistic experiences.
  • Sound Exploration – making music learning accessible and engaging.
  • Gestures – connecting sound, visuals, and gesture in immersive spaces.
  • Rehabilitation – supporting motor and cognitive training through sound and movement.
Explore a world where every movement shapes sound, expression, and human connection.

Rehabilitation

The Handscapes family of interactive musical instruments for rehabilitation. Instead of repetitive exercises, patients make music: every hand and finger movement produces sound, turning therapy into play.

  • Handwave Synth: sweep the index finger through space to shape sound sequences.
  • Fingernote: pinch to play single notes and train fine motor precision.
  • Harmonizer: sweep the hand across three zones to build chords and coordinate movements to song harmonization.
Immediate sound feedback keeps patients motivated and lets therapists see progress as it happens.
