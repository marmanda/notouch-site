For therapists and researchers

This page is dedicated to therapists and researchers who are interested in using no-touch instruments for rehabilitation and therapy. The tool in this page are designed to support the development of motor skills, cognitive abilities, and emotional well-being in individuals with various needs. The instruments are designed to be adaptable and to accomodate various degrees of difficulty in the same movement. Moreover they allow the log for every patient and the recording of the data of the sessions, which can be used for research purposes.

Tool Description Features

Fingernote_v3.16

 This interactive tool aims to enhance fine motor coordination and dexterity of both hands. A note from the C major scale is assigned to each finger and is activated when the user pinches that finger against the thumb. The volume of the sound is dynamically adjusted according to the distance of the hands from the screen, providing an engaging multimodal experience that combines motor training and musical interaction.
    Full scale (from C4 to C5, starting from the left little finger and ending with the right little finger)
  • Half scale (the first four notes for both hands)
  • Sound exploration

Dust

 An exploratory tool that invites to observe, manipulate, and interact with fine particles, revealing patterns, movements, and transformations within a constantly changing material landscape.
  • Transformation
  • Sound exploration

Liquid Threshold

 Explores liquidity as a relational state between gesture, image, and sound. Through hand tracking, each crossing of a threshold generates luminous efflorescences and aquatic resonances, transforming movement into a form of sensitive matter.
  • Self-Regulation
  • Sound exploration

Skies

 Transforms movement into wind. Through touchless interaction, performers shape invisible air currents that become sound, light, and motion. Horizontal hand movement controls the brightness of the sound, while vertical movement adjusts resonance, allowing users to sculpt the character and intensity of the airflow.
  • Material exploration
  • Movement & Transformation
