Elements

It is a collection of interactive sonic environments inspired by the four classical elements: Air, Water, Fire, and Earth. Through gesture-based interaction, each environment invites participants to explore a distinctive ecology of sounds, movements, and visual atmospheres. Rather than representing nature, Elements seeks to create immersive experiences that evoke different qualities of perception, energy, and embodiment.

Skies

Skies is inspired by the movement and dynamics of the atmosphere. Through hand gestures, participants shape evolving wind-like sounds and flowing visual landscapes, transforming the screen into a space of motion, turbulence, and sonic exploration. Horizontal movements influence the brightness and spectral qualities of the sound, while vertical gestures control resonance and intensity, allowing users to navigate a continuum ranging from gentle breezes to powerful gusts. At the same time, streams of animated particles respond to both atmospheric forces and bodily movement, creating a continuous dialogue between sound, gesture, and visual motion.

Droplets

Droplets is an interactive environment that transforms simple reaching gestures into delicate sonic and visual events inspired by water. By moving the index finger towards the screen, participants create virtual droplets that ripple across the digital space, generating shimmering sounds and expanding patterns of light. Drawing upon the physical qualities of water, the work explores fluidity, depth, and transformation through embodied interaction. Each gesture produces a unique combination of sound and movement, ranging from bright crystalline drops to deeper resonant textures. Visual particles emerge, expand, and gradually dissolve, evoking the transient nature of water itself.

Firelash

Firelash is an interactive gesture-driven environment that transforms rapid hand movements into bursts of fire, sound, and energy. Unlike touchless systems based on continuous control, Firelash is built around a single expressive action: the swipe. A swift horizontal movement across space becomes the catalyst for an instantaneous audiovisual event, turning motion into impact. Inspired by the dynamics of flame and ignition, the work explores how velocity, force, and intention can be translated into immersive sensory experiences. The speed of each gesture directly influences the intensity of the resulting fire blast, encouraging participants to experiment with different qualities of movement, from subtle flickers to powerful surges of energy.

Dust

It is inspired by the restless behavior of suspended particles. Through the movement of a single hand, participants influence a vast field of digital dust, transforming its texture, density, and motion while simultaneously shaping an evolving landscape of noise-based sound. At the heart of the work lies the relationship between order and disturbance. Thousands of particles continuously drift around the screen, maintaining a fragile equilibrium until the performer's gestures intervene. As the hand moves through space, the dust disperses, swirls, and reorganizes itself, revealing patterns that emerge from the interaction between bodily action and collective motion.
