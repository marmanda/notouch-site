Sound Exploration

Sound Exploration is a collection of interactive environments designed to investigate the relationship between gesture, sound, and perception. Through touchless interaction, participants are invited to explore sound not as a fixed musical object, but as a dynamic material that can be shaped, discovered, and experienced through movement.

Sine Rings

It is an interactive audiovisual environment that transforms hand movements into expanding circles of sound and light. Through the motion of a single finger, participants generate evolving fields of pure sine tones whose frequencies, resonances, and visual traces continuously unfold across space. Vertical movements shape pitch and timbral brightness, while each gesture leaves behind luminous concentric rings that slowly expand and dissolve, revealing the temporal traces of interaction.

Crackling Sounds

Crackling Sounds explores the sonic and visual qualities of friction, electricity, and intermittent energy. Through touchless hand interaction, participants shape evolving fields of crackling noise, resonant tones, and luminous sparks that emerge and dissolve in real time. The work is inspired by phenomena such as burning embers, electrical discharges, and the subtle textures of unstable matter. The position of the hand influences both the spectral character of the sound and the behavior of the visual environment, generating a continuous interplay between smooth resonance and granular disruption.
