Gestures

Handscapes Gestures explores how simple, recognizable hand actions can become meaningful sonic events. Rather than relying on continuous control, this family of interactions is built around discrete gestures that trigger, transform, or release sound through direct bodily action. The works gathered in this section investigate gesture as a shared language between body and technology. Actions such as crossing a threshold, producing a burst of energy, or connecting virtual structures become immediate sources of audiovisual feedback, allowing participants to engage with sound through intuitive and expressive movements.

Liquid Thresholds

Liquid Thresholds focuses on the subtle moment in which a gesture crosses an invisible boundary. This passage gives rise to fluid transformations of sound and image, revealing how a seemingly simple action can alter the character of an entire environment. Inspired by the behavior of water, the work explores thresholds not as barriers, but as moments of emergence and transformation.

Bursts

Bursts focuses on sudden releases of energy. Specific gestures generate explosive audiovisual events that emerge, expand, and rapidly dissipate through space. Inspired by phenomena such as sparks, pulses, and eruptions, the environment transforms intentional actions into instantaneous expressions of force, creating a direct relationship between movement and impact.

Sticks

Sticks investigates mid-air touch as a trigger for audiovisual interaction. Using real-time hand tracking, the system detects moments of contact between the index fingers of both hands and maps these events to sample-based sound generation and visual particle systems. The project explores how discrete gestures of connection can support intuitive, accessible, and embodied forms of interaction.
